# Glossary

## Post
A single Instagram publication the user is designing. A Post is either a Collage or a Carousel, and has one Aspect Ratio.

## Collage
A Post that is exported as one image, composed of several photos arranged by a Layout.

## Carousel
A Post exported as an ordered sequence of up to 20 Slides, which viewers swipe through.

## Slide
One image within a Carousel. All Slides of a Carousel share the Post's Aspect Ratio.

## Seamless Carousel
A Carousel designed as one continuous wide canvas that is cut into Slides at export, so content can span the boundary between adjacent Slides.

## Aspect Ratio
The width-to-height proportion of a Post's images. Supported for every Post: 1:1 (square), 4:5 (portrait, the default) and 3:4 (tall portrait). 9:16 is allowed only for a Story.

## Story
A Collage at 9:16, made for Instagram Stories. Not a separate kind of Post; a Carousel is never a Story.

## Grid Crop
The centred 3:4 portion of a Post's first image that Instagram's profile grid shows. A 3:4 Post loses nothing to it.

## Layout
The arrangement of photo cells within a Collage or Slide. A Template supplies a starting Layout.

## Template
A reusable, pre-designed starting point for a Post — Layout plus styling — that the user fills with their own photos.

_Avoid_: "slides" to mean the whole product output; say Carousel (the post) or Slide (one image of it).
